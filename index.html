How it works

Four steps, no middleman.

Everything runs in the browser tab you already have open. Cloak has no server to trust, no account to make and no one to email about your keys.

STEP 01

Open a pocket

Choose a passphrase. Cloak creates a private pocket account and locks its key with AES-256 before anything is saved.

STEP 02

Drop it in

Send SOL from Phantom into the pocket. Cloak writes a sealed note to your vault: a fingerprint of the amount and two random secrets.

STEP 03

Leave by a new door

Withdraw to new addresses that have never appeared on-chain, split into random amounts and sent in separate transactions.

STEP 04

Show, don't tell

Export a read-only view key that lists your notes without their secrets. Hand it to an accountant and they can audit you without being able to spend.

Under the hood

Boring cryptography, on purpose.

Cloak uses only standard, well-tested building blocks that already ship in your browser. Nothing is homemade.

vault.js — runs locally
// 1. your passphrase becomes a key
key = PBKDF2(passphrase, salt, 310_000, SHA-256)

// 2. the vault is sealed with it
vault = AES-GCM-256(key, iv, {
  pocket, notes, freshKeys
})

// 3. every deposit gets a sealed note
note = SHA-256(amount ‖ secret ‖ nullifier)

// 4. every exit gets a brand-new keypair
fresh = ed25519.generate()

FAQ

Fair questions.

Is there a Cloak token?

No. There's no token, presale, airdrop or "early access" list. If someone offers you one, it isn't from this project.

Can you see or freeze my funds?

No. Cloak has no server, so there's nothing to see and no switch to flip. Your pocket key exists only inside your encrypted vault, which also means nobody can recover it if you lose your passphrase and your backup.

Why only devnet?

Today the hop into your pocket is still visible on-chain. Running on mainnet before the shared pool and the audit exist would give people a false sense of safety. Devnet SOL is free, so you can try every feature without risk.

Which wallets work?

Phantom on desktop, or Phantom's in-app browser on mobile. Turn on Testnet Mode in Phantom (Settings → Developer Settings) so your balance shows devnet SOL.

Can I run my own copy?

Yes. Clone the repo and serve the folder with any static host. There's no build step and nothing to configure.
