The problem

Pay a friend, a freelancer or a shop once, and they can see your balance, every trade you've made, and every wallet you've ever touched. Explorers and analytics firms index all of it.

How it works

Four moves. Keys never leave your browser.

Cloak runs in your browser. It encrypts its own key material with a passphrase only you know, and it never sends that key material anywhere.

FOLD

Open a pocket

Create an encrypted vault in your browser. It holds the keys for your pocket account and every fresh address you make.

SEAL

Move funds in

Send SOL from Phantom into your pocket. Cloak records a sealed note: a hash commitment to the amount plus a secret.

SCATTER

Step out fresh

Withdraw to brand-new addresses in amounts you choose. They have no history and are never reused.

PROVE

Share on your terms

Export a read-only view key listing your notes without their secrets, for an accountant or for yourself.

Honest privacy

What Cloak hides today, and what it doesn't yet.

Privacy tools that overpromise get people hurt. Here's where things stand right now.

Hidden now

  • Address reuseEach withdrawal goes to a never-used address.
  • Your keysGenerated and encrypted locally (AES-GCM, PBKDF2).
  • Note secretsKept in your vault only, never sent to a server.
  • TrackingNo analytics, no cookies, no backend.

Not yet hidden

  • Pocket linkWithout a shared pool, your wallet → pocket → fresh hop is visible on-chain.
  • AmountsSOL transfer amounts are public. Splitting and timing only help a little.
  • IP addressRPC calls show your IP. Use a VPN or your own RPC.
  • AuditCloak has not been audited. Don't trust it with funds that matter.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Is there a Cloak token?

No. Cloak doesn't have a token, a presale or an airdrop. Anyone selling one is not us.

Does Cloak hold my funds?

No. Your pocket account's key is created in your browser and encrypted with your passphrase. If you lose the passphrase and haven't exported a backup, nobody can recover it.

Why devnet only?

Until the shared pool and the audit are done, using real money would give a false sense of privacy. Devnet SOL is free and can't be lost.

Which wallets work?

Phantom today. Any wallet that injects the standard Solana provider should work too.
